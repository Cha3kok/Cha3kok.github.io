Starting as an IPTV Reseller: Panel Notes and the Markets You Can Serve

Updated 8 November 2026

Reselling internet TV means your reputation depends on someone else's servers. Before buying credits, it helps to know what a reseller panel offers and which country-specific services your customers might compare you with.

Reseller panel

  • For people who resell IPTV: IPTV Panel Resellers — about ; trial: ask support; refunds: not activated only.

IPTV Panel Resellers: Credits never expire. White-label panel to create customer accounts. One caveat: Not for home viewers.

Services your US customers will compare

  • Best overall for US viewers: Golden Gate IPTV — from $20 a month; trial: on request; refunds: 7 days.
  • Best for long US plans: Multivision IPTV — from $19.99 a month; trial: 3 hours, free; refunds: 48 hours.
  • Cheapest first month in the USA: GoldenGateIPTV.us — from $9.99 a month; trial: 24 hours, free; refunds: 48 hours.
  • Best for multi-screen homes: IPTV VIP — from $20 a month; trial: on request; refunds: 7 days.
  • Best for 2 to 3 screens: IPTV Subscription — from $20 a month; trial: not offered; refunds: 7 days.
  • Best refund policy: Apollo Group TV — from $15.99 a month; trial: 3 hours, free; refunds: 7 days, any reason.
  • Best for a full-day free trial: IPTV Trends — from $19.99 a month; trial: 24 hours, free; refunds: 7 days.

Golden Gate IPTV: Six plan lengths, from a $7 day pass to 24 months. 24 months works out at about $4.96 a month. One caveat: Trial length is at the provider's discretion.

Multivision IPTV: 24 months for $129.99 (about $5.42 a month). Free 3-hour trial without a card. One caveat: Short 48-hour refund window.

GoldenGateIPTV.us: Lowest one-month price in our US list: $9.99. Free 24-hour trial, no card. One caveat: Short 48-hour refund window.

IPTV VIP: Clear prices for 1 to 5 screens. $7 day pass to test on your own TV. One caveat: Orders and payment links go through WhatsApp.

IPTV Subscription: Yearly plans for 1, 2 or 3 connections. 7-day money-back guarantee. One caveat: No free trial.

Apollo Group TV: Refund for any reason within 7 days. Partial refund after 7 days for unused time. One caveat: Access ends as soon as you cancel.

IPTV Trends: Free 24-hour trial. 7-day refund. One caveat: Monthly price is on the high side at $19.99.

Services your UK and Canadian customers will compare

  • Best value in the UK: IPTV United Kingdom — from £10 a month; trial: 24 hours, free; refunds: 7 days.
  • UK plans for up to 4 devices: British IPTV — from £15 a month; trial: 3 hours, free; refunds: 48 hours.
  • Best UK refund terms: BenTV Pro UK — from £15 a month; trial: free, on request; refunds: 7 days.
  • Best for Canada (English and French): IPTVCA — from CA$19 a month; trial: 24 hours, free; refunds: only if it never works.
  • Cheapest first month in Canada: ControlYourself.ca — from CA$13.99 a month; trial: 24 hours, free; refunds: 48 hours.
  • Best Canadian two-year plan: BenTV Pro Canada — from CA$13.99 a month; trial: 24 hours, free; refunds: 48 hours.

IPTV United Kingdom: Lowest UK prices here: £10 a month, £49 a year. Free 24-hour trial. One caveat: Refund only when the service does not work and cannot be fixed.

British IPTV: Plans for 1 to 4 devices. Free trial without a card. One caveat: Short 3-hour trial.

BenTV Pro UK: 7-day refund after activation if it will not work. Orders checked by a person, not a bot. One caveat: Trial length is not given.

IPTVCA: Full French version for Quebec. 3 months for CA$29. One caveat: Strict refund policy once activated.

ControlYourself.ca: CA$13.99 for one month. Free 24-hour trial. One caveat: Yearly plan costs more than IPTVCA's.

BenTV Pro Canada: 24 months for CA$159 (about CA$6.63 a month). Free 24-hour trial. One caveat: 48-hour refund window.

Reseller checklist

  • Credit price and minimum order.
  • Trial lines for your own testing.
  • How support handles tickets from your customers.
  • Panel uptime during big sporting events.

Start small, test the panel during a busy weekend, and grow only when your first customers are happy.
